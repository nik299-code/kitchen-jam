Allergens: checked, not promised.

Tell it what you avoid, and every recipe is checked for common allergens — including hidden sources.

  • Worcestershire saucefish
  • Ordinary soy saucewheat

It is a check on the AI’s work, not a guarantee. Always read labels and verify ingredients yourself.

No account. No server of ours in the middle.

On iPhones and iPads with Apple Intelligence running iOS 27, recipes are written by Apple Intelligence on Apple’s Private Cloud Compute, with no key. Your request goes to Apple to be answered — Apple states it keeps none of it — and never to us. We never see your recipes, your pantry, or your prompts.

No Apple Intelligence? Connect your own AI instead. The app walks you through it.
