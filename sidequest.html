Thanksgiving Side Quest: official rules

A side-dish contest on Instagram · 20–27 November 2026

No purchase or payment of any kind is needed to enter or win.

This contest is not sponsored, endorsed or run by, or associated with, Instagram or Meta.

  1. Sponsor. The maker of the Kitchen Jam app (“we”), an independent developer based in the United States. The contest runs on the Instagram account that posted the Thanksgiving Side Quest announcement (“the contest account”). Contact: hello@mykitchenjam.app.
  2. Dates. Entries open Friday 20 November 2026 at 9:00 am PT and close Friday 27 November 2026 at 11:59 pm PT. We announce winners on or about Monday 30 November 2026.
  3. Who can enter. Legal residents of the 50 United States and DC who are 18 or older when they enter. Our own household and anyone who helps run the contest cannot enter.
  4. How to enter. Post to a public Instagram account, during the dates above, a side dish with:
    • a photo of the dish or a screenshot of its recipe, and
    • the hashtag #KitchenJamSideQuest, and a tag of the contest account, and
    • the badge you are going for: Most Innovative, Quirkiest, Most Modernist, Laziest or (from Friday 27 November) Best Leftover Glow-up.

    You may make the recipe with Kitchen Jam, but you do not have to: a recipe of your own enters the same way. Up to one entry per badge per person. The dish and photo must be your own.

  5. How we pick winners. This is a contest of skill, not chance. For each badge, our team picks one winner on: fit to the badge (40%), creativity (40%) and how good it looks or reads (20%). We may run Instagram Story polls for fun; the polls do not decide the winners. If a badge gets no entries that meet these rules, it has no winner.
  6. Prize. Each winner (up to five) gets:
    • their recipe in the Kitchen Jam app: we add it to the example recipes that every user’s Library receives, in the next app update after the contest (expected December 2026). It shows a credit line, “Side Quest winner · @handle”, if the winner chooses; otherwise “Side Quest winner”;
    • an original drawing of their dish in Kitchen Jam’s dish-art style, used in the app and sent to the winner as a digital file; and
    • a feature on the contest account.

    Users get the recipe only when they install the update. We do not promise a release date: App Store review and our release schedule decide it. If we cannot ship a winning recipe in the app (for example, it fails our check in rule 8), the winner still gets the drawing and the feature. Approximate retail value: $0 (no cash value). No substitution or transfer.

  7. Winner notice. We contact winners by Instagram direct message from the contest account only. We will never ask for a password, payment or bank details. A winner who does not reply within 7 days loses the prize, and we may pick the next-ranked entry.
  8. Your entry. You keep ownership of your photo and recipe. By entering, you let us repost your entry, with credit to your handle, on our social accounts and website, for 12 months. We draw the prize from your dish; you may use the drawing as you like, and so may we.

    If you win, we ask you, in the winner message, to agree in writing that we may include your recipe and the drawing in the Kitchen Jam app, without time limit and without payment, and to say whether the app may show your handle. Before it ships we may rewrite the recipe into the app’s format (steps, quantities, timings), check it and test it. Like every recipe in the app, it gets an allergen check and a nutrition estimate, which are checks, not guarantees. If you do not agree, you still get the drawing and the feature, and your recipe stays out of the app.

  9. Rules for content. No entries that are offensive, unsafe, copied from someone else, or that show brands or people who did not agree. We may disqualify any entry that breaks these rules or Instagram’s terms.
  10. Privacy. We use your Instagram handle only to run this contest and to credit you. We do not collect other personal data for it.
  11. General. We may change or cancel the contest if something outside our control stops it from running fairly. Void where prohibited. Questions: hello@mykitchenjam.app.
